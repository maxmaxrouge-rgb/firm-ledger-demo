LEDGER LENS / DRAFT OFFER

A clear list of reconciliation exceptions.

For Australian accounting and bookkeeping firms reviewing a small business client's ledger against bank transactions.

Proposed paid pilot

The proposed service compares one client's ledger CSV and bank CSV for one calendar quarter, up to 10,000 rows per file.

Proposed deliverables: a matched-transaction summary, an exceptions CSV with unmatched ledger and bank rows, and a short review note that explains the matching rules and items that need the firm's attention.

The firm reviews each exception and decides any accounting changes. The pilot does not include bank access, bookkeeping entries, BAS lodgement, tax advice or an accounting system migration.
